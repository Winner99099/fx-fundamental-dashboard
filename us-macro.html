How to read the US macro backdrop

Context framework for USD and major FX analysis — not an automatic trading signal.

Inflation & Federal Reserve expectations

Core CPI and Core PCE: persistent upside surprises can delay rate cuts and support front-end yields and USD. A sustained decline can have the opposite effect. Compare actual releases with consensus and prior revisions; the level alone is not a surprise signal.

Employment & growth resilience

Nonfarm Payrolls: stronger job creation can support USD when it reinforces higher-for-longer rate expectations. Also check unemployment, average hourly earnings, participation and revisions. Payrolls are reported in thousands; the monthly change is calculated from the payroll level.

2-Year Treasury yield

Policy expectations: the 2-year yield is generally more sensitive to expected Fed policy over the near term. A rising 2-year yield relative to peers can support USD, but risk sentiment and positioning also matter.

10-Year Treasury yield & DXY

10-Year TIPS real yield: this is the inflation-adjusted real yield on Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities. Higher real yields can support USD by improving the relative return on dollar assets. DXY measures USD against a fixed six-currency basket, heavily weighted to EUR. Confirm direction across EURUSD, USDJPY and broad risk conditions.

Core CPI and payrolls use BLS data; Core PCE uses the BEA series through a public USD data endpoint; The 10-Year TIPS Real Yield uses the US Treasury Daily Par Real Yield Curve feed; the 2-Year nominal yield and 10Y–2Y spread use the nominal Treasury curve. The nominal 10-Year yield is retained in the underlying data for the nominal yield-curve spread, but is no longer shown in the main card. The DXY figure is calculated from the classic six-currency basket using same-day ECB euro reference rates. It is a daily DXY proxy, not the official ICE futures close or a real-time quote. Monthly economic indicators update only when agencies publish new observations. Daily yields do not update on weekends or US market holidays.
