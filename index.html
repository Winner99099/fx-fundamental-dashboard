Scoring methodology

V3 does not rank currencies from policy-rate levels alone or invent missing macro data. V3.3 compares the latest annual inflation, GDP-growth and unemployment readings with up to five preceding annual observations. Inflation and unemployment below their own recent history, and GDP growth above its own recent history, contribute positively. Available component weights (25% inflation, 20% GDP, 15% labour) are renormalised. Policy-rate outlook (30%), central-bank communication (10%), consensus surprises and market confirmation are not yet scored. Inflation momentum is not the same as distance from a central-bank target. Scores are partial research context, not trade signals.
