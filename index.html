How to interpret the three readings

Structural bias: the slower-moving relative macro score. Short-term momentum: a simple Strong, Neutral or Weak classification based on recent changes in GDP growth, unemployment and inflation relative to target. Momentum persistence: an evidence-based label reflecting the number, directional agreement and spacing of recent scored catalysts, with older catalysts fading. A single surprise is not treated as a durable trend. The event overlay has a 36-hour half-life and a seven-day observation window. This is a research aid, not a standalone trade signal.

How bias is derived

Bias = base short-term score minus quote short-term score. Above +0.35 is bullish, below −0.35 is bearish, between them is neutral. This is a relative macro read, not a price forecast.
