TRANSPARENCY & USER PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

DRAFT — publisher contact details and final SDK/data review required before publication · 13 November 2026

This policy explains how MacroPulseFX handles information when you use the MacroPulseFX website or Android application. MacroPulseFX is a market-research and educational dashboard. It does not execute trades, hold customer funds, or provide brokerage services.

1. Information we collect

The current version does not require user registration and does not ask you to provide a name, address, financial account credentials, broker login, or payment information.

The Trading Journal feature saves entries in your browser's local storage on the device where you use it. Journal entries are not transmitted to MacroPulseFX servers by the current application. They may be lost if you clear browser/app storage, uninstall the app, or change devices. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, or other sensitive information into journal notes.

2. Data used to provide the dashboard

The application retrieves public macroeconomic, central-bank and economic-calendar information from the configured data service and links to external information providers. The dashboard may use GitHub Pages hosting and Google Fonts. Your device may make requests to these providers when loading the app or its content. Those providers process technical request information under their own privacy policies.

3. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current build does not intentionally include an advertising SDK, behavioural advertising, or third-party analytics SDK. If analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are added in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be reviewed and updated before release.

4. Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising and direct sponsorship are planned for a future release but are not active in the current build. No advertising SDK, ad tag, sponsor tracking pixel or advertising partner is currently integrated. Before any advertising or sponsorship is activated, this policy will be updated to identify the providers, explain any cookies or device identifiers used, describe what information is collected or shared, explain advertising personalisation choices, and provide any required consent and privacy controls. Sponsored placements will be clearly labelled and kept separate from independent macroeconomic data and analysis.

5. Data sharing

MacroPulseFX does not sell personal information. The current Trading Journal entries remain in local browser storage and are not shared by the application. External hosting, font, data and linked website providers may receive technical connection information when your device accesses their services.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored journal entries within the app or clear the app/browser storage on your device. Uninstalling the Android app may remove locally stored app data according to Android's storage behaviour. Public macroeconomic snapshots are not personal user data.

7. Security

Use a device lock and keep your operating system and browser up to date. The app is a research tool and should not be used to store trading passwords, brokerage credentials, bank details, or other secrets. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

MacroPulseFX is designed as a general financial research tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features, hosting, data providers, analytics, or advertising arrangements change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised date.

10. Contact

A dedicated publisher privacy-contact email address must be inserted here before Google Play submission. Do not use a public issue tracker for private data or account information.

11. Important information

MacroPulseFX provides general market data and educational research only. It is not personalised investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell a financial instrument, or a substitute for independent professional advice. Market data may be delayed, incomplete, revised, or unavailable.
